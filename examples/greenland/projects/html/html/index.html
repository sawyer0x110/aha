From observation to mechanism

Why Mercator makes Greenland look enormous

Africa has about fourteen times Greenland's reported area. A Mercator map can obscure that comparison because its magnification increases toward the poles.

Separate land area from projected area

Spherical Mercator; local scale is not a whole-country multiplier. Generalized boundaries are not statistical area measurements.

About fourteen times, not fourteen packed outlines

The cited areas are 2,166,086 km² for Greenland and about 30,365,000 km² for Africa. They support a rounded ratio, not a claim that fourteen Greenland outlines fit inside Africa. The official Greenland PDF body was not verified.

Equal longitude gaps are not equal ground distances

Meridians converge on a sphere but stay equally spaced on a Mercator map. The map therefore stretches east-west distances more at high latitude. To preserve local angles, it stretches north-south distances by the same local factor.

At 60°: twice in each direction, four times in area

For a tiny patch in spherical Mercator with equatorial scale one, linear scale is sec(latitude) and area scale is its square. Greenland spans many latitudes: a local fourfold factor is not a whole-country multiplier.

Move the land on a sphere, not by shrinking a picture

Apply the same three-dimensional rotation to every boundary vertex. Spherical area stays unchanged while its outline on a fixed Mercator map changes. Natural Earth 1:110 million boundaries are generalized public-domain data, not precise survey measurements.

Choose the property your question needs

Mercator preserves local angles and draws constant-heading rhumb lines straight; they are usually not shortest routes. Its poles lie at infinity and must be cropped. Equal Earth preserves area proportions, but not every shape, angle or distance.

从现象到机制

墨卡托地图为什么把格陵兰放大了？

按引用的面积统计，非洲约为格陵兰的 14 倍。墨卡托地图越靠近两极放大越严重，因此看轮廓容易误判面积比例。

把土地面积与地图上的面积分开

球面墨卡托；局部倍率不是整国倍率。概化边界的计算面积不替代统计面积。

约 14 倍，不是装进 14 个轮廓

引用资料给出格陵兰 2,166,086 km²、非洲约 30,365,000 km²。它们支持约 14 倍的比较，而非轮廓装箱；本次研究未核验格陵兰官方 PDF 的正文。

同样的经度间隔，不是同样的地面距离

球面上的经线向两极汇聚，墨卡托图上的经线却始终等距，因此高纬地区东西方向被放大。为了保持局部角度，南北方向也要按相同的局部倍率拉伸。

60° 附近：两方向各两倍，面积四倍

以赤道比例为 1 的球面墨卡托为例，微小区域的线性倍率是纬度余弦的倒数，面积倍率是其平方。格陵兰跨越多个纬度，不能把局部四倍当成整国倍率。

在球面上移动土地，而不是缩小图片

对边界的所有顶点施加同一个三维旋转：球面面积保持不变，固定墨卡托图上的轮廓却会改变。Natural Earth 1:1.1 亿边界是公共领域概化数据，不是精密测量。

先问地图要保留什么

墨卡托保留局部角度，把恒定罗盘航向的恒向线画成直线，但它通常不是最短路线；两极在无穷远，有限地图必须裁切。Equal Earth 保面积比例，却不保所有形状、角度和距离。
