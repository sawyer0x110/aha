Aha · research → explanation

How does Aha turn research into an explanation?

Aha separates finding a supported answer from designing how someone will understand it. Two portable skills share a research snapshot; each requested medium gets its own authored source.

01 / Start with the reader’s question

A report is an answer. A visual is another act of authorship.

Tell the host agent what you want to understand, what materials it may use, and what you intend to do with the answer. A report-only request stops after research. A visual request follows the explanation workflow, doing research first when needed; it does not depend on automatic dispatch between skills.

aha-research

Investigate and preserve

Read sources, test competing explanations against evidence, keep uncertainty visible. Deliver an independent report plus a Research Dossier.

aha-explain

Author for the chosen medium

Reuse sufficiently fresh research, choose the visual relationships, author real source, then check and review the requested output.

02 / A case where interaction computes

ANC: change the phase, then see the sum.

Solid: incomingDashed: speakerHeavy: sum
0.00Residual peak / incoming peak

At ideal opposition, the equal pressures cancel.

The control changes a condition, not a caption.

The existing ANC example computes three SVG curves when its phase slider moves: incoming pressure, speaker pressure, and their sum. This original redraw exposes that same ideal-model relationship. The two input amplitudes stay equal; only their phase relationship changes.

At 60° mismatch, the residual peak equals the incoming peak. At 180°, it is twice the incoming peak—not twice the perceived loudness. This is neither headset telemetry nor a prediction of a real headphone’s attenuation.

04 / Share evidence, not geometry

Four media answer four reading situations.

HTML

Let readers choose a condition, inspect a record, and expand a source note. English and Chinese are authored branches, not automatic translation.

PNG

Compose one self-contained reading path. Select dimensions from the user’s explicit size, destination and display width—not from the scaffold. More pixels do not enlarge text at the same displayed width.

PPTX

Use editable text, shapes and charts to reveal relationships in sequence. A whole-slide screenshot would remove the very edits a presenter needs.

Narrated video

Show the condition change and its consequence over time, with deterministic frames and caption space. A sequence of fading pages is not a mechanism explanation.

Source: medium-specific authoring and updated image sizing

05 / Know what the machine checks

A matching hash is a chain of custody, not a verdict.

Runtime implements

Dossier validation; research/source binding; declared coverage; offline HTML packaging; approved capture or native generation; output receipts. Video uses browser frames and FFmpeg: 1280 × 720, 30 fps, H.264/AAC, with segment-level captions.

Host and reviewers provide

Research judgment, source retrieval, translation, clear causal writing, resource permission, execution consent and actual visual or listening review. Native objects do not prove convenient editing; sampled frame changes do not prove meaningful motion.

Why a changed source can invalidate old audio

A video plan binds narration, provider, voice, rate, research and visual source. The current audio manifest binds the complete plan. Editing the source therefore changes the plan identity. Revalidate and obtain current-plan approval; do not patch hashes to reuse old audio. Edge TTS sends approved narration to an online service, so that permission is separate from local rendering.

Aha · 研究 → 解释

Aha 怎样把研究变成可解释的视觉作品？

Aha 把“找到有依据的答案”和“设计让人理解答案的方式”分开。两个可移植技能共用研究快照，但每一种目标媒介都需要独立创作。

01 / 从读者的问题开始

报告先给出答案；视觉解释还需要再创作。

先告诉宿主智能体：你想弄懂什么、允许使用哪些材料、准备怎样使用答案。只要报告，就停在研究阶段；需要视觉作品，则按解释工作流先补足研究。这里不假定宿主会自动在两个技能之间转发任务。

aha-research

调查，并保留依据

读取来源，用证据比较不同解释，保留不确定性。交付独立报告和 Research Dossier（研究档案）。

aha-explain

针对所选媒介创作

复用足够新鲜的研究，选择要呈现的关系，创作真实源码，再检查和审阅用户要求的产物。

  1. 1 · 问题用途与范围
  2. 2 · 研究论点、证据与缺口
  3. 3 · 快照固定的研究身份
  4. 4 · 创作媒介特定源码
  5. 5 · 审阅许可 → 渲染 → 实际 QA

02 / 一个真正进行计算的交互案例

ANC：改变相位，再看声压之和。

细实线：传入虚线：扬声器粗线：相加结果
0.00残余峰值 / 传入峰值

理想反向时，等幅声压相消。

控件改变的是条件，不只是说明文字。

现有 ANC 示例在拖动相位滑块时，计算传入声压、扬声器声压及两者之和三条 SVG 曲线。这里重新绘制这个理想模型：两路幅值保持相等，只改变它们的相位关系。

偏差为 60° 时，残余峰值等于传入峰值；180° 时为两倍，但不能说“听起来两倍响”。这不是耳机遥测，也不预测真实耳机的降噪量。

04 / 共享证据，不照搬几何

四种媒介对应四种阅读方式。

HTML

让读者选择条件、检查记录、展开来源说明。英文和中文分别创作，不依靠自动翻译。

PNG

在单张图中组织完整阅读路径。先看用户指定尺寸、目标容器和显示宽度，不把脚手架当设计建议。同一显示宽度下，更多像素不会让字变大。

PPTX

用可编辑文本、形状和图表逐步呈现关系。整页截图会剥夺演讲者真正需要的编辑能力。

旁白视频

用确定性帧随时间显示条件变化及其后果，同时给字幕留空间。整页淡入淡出的序列不等于机制解释。

来源：媒介特定创作与新版图像尺寸指南

05 / 分清机器检查了什么

哈希吻合说明身份一致，不代表内容正确。

运行时实现

Dossier 校验、研究与源码绑定、声明覆盖、离线 HTML 打包、经许可的捕获或原生生成、输出回执。视频用浏览器帧与 FFmpeg：1280 × 720、30fps、H.264/AAC，以及片段级字幕。

宿主与审阅者负责

研究判断、来源获取、翻译、清楚的因果表达、资源授权、执行许可，以及实际看图和听音频。原生对象不证明好编辑，抽样帧发生变化也不证明动画有意义。

为什么改了画面源码，也会让旧音频绑定失效？

视频计划绑定旁白、提供方、声音、语速、研究和视觉源码；当前音频清单又绑定完整计划。因此改源码会改变计划身份。应重新校验并取得当前计划许可，不应修改哈希强行复用旧音频。Edge TTS 会把获批旁白发给在线服务，这与本地渲染许可分开。
