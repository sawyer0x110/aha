From observation to mechanism

Why noise cancellation leaves some voices audible

ANC adds a second sound pressure at your ear. Opposing waves can reduce the residual; ideal complete cancellation needs equal amplitudes and opposite phase at that position. It does not classify sounds as aircraft or voices.

Follow the pressure, not the sound label

Ideal pressure model at one target position. Not measured decibels, perceived loudness, or a prediction for your headphones.

Two paths, one listening position

Incoming noise and the loudspeaker output travel different acoustic paths. The controller must account for what reaches the ear, not merely flip the sign of the outside microphone signal.

A small timing error matters more at higher frequency

For an assumed uncompensated error of 0.10 ms, the phase error is 3.6° at 100 Hz and 36° at 1,000 Hz. This is a single-tone calculation, not a headphone measurement or universal frequency cutoff.

The seal blocks a path; ANC adds pressure

Passive isolation changes how sound enters. Active control adds another pressure wave. Switching ANC off leaves the physical barrier in place; taking the headphones off changes both.

Partly reduced can still mean audible

Speech spans many frequency components. Some may be reduced while others remain. Recognizable residual speech does not prove that no speech was attenuated, or that its pressure was amplified.

Fit and mode can change the comparison

A seal leak changes the acoustic path. Transparency deliberately admits outside sound; adaptive modes can change behavior. Keep fit and sound source fixed when comparing ANC on and off. That is a proposed comparison, not a test performed here.

从现象到机制

降噪耳机为什么仍能听见人声？

主动降噪在耳边加入控制声压，抵消部分传入波动。理想的完全抵消才要求目标位置等幅反相；部分减弱不要求幅度完全相等。它不是飞机与人声的分类器。

沿着声压的路径看，而不是按声音类别判断

同一目标位置的理想声压模型；不是实测分贝、感知响度，也不能预测具体耳机的表现。

两条路径，在同一个位置相加

外界噪声和扬声器发出的声音经过不同传播路径。控制器需要考虑最后到达耳边的声压，而不只是把外部麦克风读数翻转符号。

同样的时间误差，在高频中占更大一圈

假定未补偿的时间误差为 0.10 ms，100 Hz 的相位误差为 3.6°，1,000 Hz 则为 36°。这是单频解析示例，不是耳机实测，更不是所有耳机的统一失效频率。

密封改变传声，主动控制加入声压

被动隔音改变声音传入的路径，主动控制则加入另一股声压。关闭 ANC 后，耳罩或耳塞的物理屏障仍在；摘下耳机则同时改变了两者。

部分减弱，并不等于听不见

语音包含多个频率成分，一部分可能被削弱，另一部分仍然残留。还能辨认出说话，不代表语音完全未衰减，也不能据此认定语音声压被放大。

佩戴与模式也会改变结果

密封泄漏会改变声学路径。通透模式主动引入环境声，自适应模式也可能改变行为。比较 ANC 开关时，应尽量保持佩戴和声源一致；这是比较建议，并非此处执行的测试。
