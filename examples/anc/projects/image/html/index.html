Why voices remain after aircraft rumble fades

Active noise cancellation reduces pressure at the ear—not a category called “noise.”
Parts of speech can be quieter and still be audible.

One meeting point. Two pressure contributions.

Passive barriers change transmission, often helping higher frequencies. ANC adds pressure, often helping lower ones. ANC off leaves the barrier in place.
