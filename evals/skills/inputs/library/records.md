# Evening collection desk records

Original synthetic editorial fixture. Cedar Library and all records below are invented for evaluation, not real municipal data.

[L1] The desk logs show 40 of 100 reserved books were collected during week 1; 58 of 100 during week 2. These are different reservations, not the same 100 books measured twice.

[L2] At the start of week 2 the library changed reminder wording from “Your book is ready” to “Your book is ready; reply if you need more time.” On that same Monday it extended the collection deadline from three days to five and opened the desk two evenings later than usual.

[L3] Logs contain reservation and collection totals only. They do not identify which patrons received or read reminders, whether messages were delivered, collection day, reasons for collecting, or returning patrons. There was no unchanged comparison branch and no randomized allocation.

[L4] A manager wrote in the internal newsletter: “The friendlier message made patrons feel welcome, so they collected their books.” No interviews or survey accompanied this statement.

[L5] The editor can ask for one additional month of prospective data collection. Nothing in this packet records results of such a follow-up.
