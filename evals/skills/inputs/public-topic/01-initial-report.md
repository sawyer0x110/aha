# Canal Ward evening shade pilot — initial bulletin

ORIGINAL SYNTHETIC EVALUATION MATERIAL. Canal Ward, its offices, people and measurements are fictional. This is not a real research dataset or a retrieved web page.

Document ID CW-01; issued 2031-06-03; author: fictional Canal Ward Streets Office.

[B1] The office placed removable shade panels beside four bus stops for seven weekday evenings in May. Four other stops retained their usual shelters. Staff recorded waiting-area air temperature at 17:00.

[B2] The pilot sheet reports a mean of 29.8 °C at panel stops and 31.8 °C at comparison stops: “two degrees cooler.” The office calls the panels a promising way to make waits safer during heat.

[B3] Volunteers submitted 18 comfort cards at panel stops: 13 “more comfortable,” 3 “same,” 2 “less comfortable.” There were no comfort cards at comparison stops. No passenger medical outcomes were collected.

[B4] A staff spokesperson said the office hoped to expand to 20 stops if the autumn budget allowed. This bulletin contains no budget approval.
