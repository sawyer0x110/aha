# Mesh canopy bench note

ORIGINAL SYNTHETIC EVALUATION MATERIAL; independent fictional experiment, not a published real study.

Document ID ML-07; issued 2031-06-15; fictional North Quay Materials Workshop. The workshop did not participate in the Canal Ward pilot.

[I1] In a courtyard rig, the workshop alternated a mesh canopy over one of two matched heated plates. Six 30-minute runs reversed plate positions in alternating runs. Plate surface temperature averaged 3.0 °C lower under the mesh.

[I2] Shielded air probes 1 m above each plate differed by 0.1 °C on average. Instruments, canopy mesh and courtyard differ from the Canal Ward installation.

[I3] This note reports plate and air readings only. It contains no bus passengers, comfort survey or health endpoint. It does not test the Canal Ward panels.
